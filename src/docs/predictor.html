Race Result Predictor

Evaluate a model against simple baselines and make strategy assumptions inspectable.

Engineering approach

  • Loads Jolpica race and qualifying grids with pagination, timeouts, caching and nested-response parsing.
  • Fits preprocessing and models on completed 2018–2023 races. Selects depth in early 2024, calibrates probabilities in late 2024 and evaluates 2025 once.
  • Computes form from completed prior races. Both teammates are aggregated before the constructor rolling window, preventing same-race result leakage.
  • Scores the complete available classified grid and offers a separate one-stop/two-stop scenario calculator with displayed pace, degradation, pit laps and pit loss.

Try the demo

Generate a full-grid score, inspect its qualifying baseline, then open Strategy what-if. Change pit loss or degradation to see the scenario times change. Evaluation & coverage exposes saved metadata and source cutoff.

Evidence and limits

On 2025, model position MAE is 3.388 versus qualifying baseline 3.338; probability Brier is 0.1743 versus simple baseline 0.1676 (lower is better). The model does not beat either baseline. Training-season scores are exploratory, and 2024 was used for selection/calibration. These are not live forecasts. Scenario results do not change ML probabilities. Spain 2025 has 19 entries in the source classification; an absent entry is not invented.
