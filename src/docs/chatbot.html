F1 Knowledge Assistant

Answer supported F1 questions with deterministic statistics and inspectable sources.

Engineering approach

  • Parses season, intent, driver, team and race. Unsupported years or comparisons produce a clear unavailable-data answer.
  • Includes 173 races and 3,458 classified entries from 2018–2025, final-season standings, race and qualifying results.
  • Pairs teammate qualifying only within the same team/session, shows counts and exclusions, and exports the source sessions.
  • Uses four versioned 2026 regulation summaries and three sourced circuit profiles. Topic-constrained retrieval similarity is never shown as answer confidence.

Try the demo

Ask “Who won Monaco in 2024?” or “Compare Hamilton and Russell teammate qualifying in 2024”. Open source sessions. Then ask about 2030 to see the coverage boundary. Topics & coverage opens with the keyboard; Escape closes it.

Evidence and limits

Circuit characteristics currently cover Silverstone, Monza and Monaco. Regulations are selected summaries with issue date, section, source and retrieval date, not the whole rulebook. Historical numeric coverage ends in 2025. The optional Ollama explanation is labelled separately from deterministic answers.
