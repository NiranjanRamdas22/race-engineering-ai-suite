F1 Telemetry Lab

Compare complete laps without misleading alignment or invented measurements.

Engineering approach

  • Selects one accurate, non-deleted, non-pit lap per driver. FastF1 nGear and boolean braking are handled explicitly.
  • Sorts and deduplicates samples, aligns the shared distance interval and uses elapsed time at matching distances. Missing sectors remain unavailable.
  • Uses circuit metadata for corner names. Select a corner row or the corner selector to highlight the map and traces.
  • Shows pace by elapsed session time with compound, stint and rain filters. Dry comparable race stints need at least five valid laps for an observed pace slope and uncertainty.

Try the demo

Choose 2024 → Silverstone → Q. Keep Russell and Hamilton at their fastest valid laps and select Analyse. Open Corners, select T9, then open Track map. A saved analysis survives other interface changes.

Evidence and limits

The slope includes fuel, traffic and driver effects. It is not isolated tyre degradation or a physical rubbering-in index. Circuit markers are approximate. Official lap timing and telemetry end delta can differ because distance endpoints and integration differ. First-time session downloads require internet; cached sessions can be reused.
